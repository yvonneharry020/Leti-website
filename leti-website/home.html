How It Works

Three Steps. Zero Stress.

Whether you're booking a stay or hiring a professional — Leti makes it simple, secure, and fast.

1

Sign Up & Verify

Create your account in minutes. Verify your identity with BVN and a valid ID. This keeps everyone on the platform safe and accountable.

2
