📈 The Opportunity

A Growing Market with No Real Structure

Nigeria's short-let industry is booming — but it's chaotic. Leti is the infrastructure layer that brings order, trust, and revenue to every transaction.

The Market Today

Nigeria's short-let and home services market is expanding rapidly, but it remains:

  • Unstructured — no centralised platform for bookings or services
  • Unsafe — no identity verification, no payment protection
  • Fragmented — agents, WhatsApp groups, and scattered listings
  • Unregulated — no accountability, no dispute resolution

This isn't just a gap — it's a massive, revenue-generating opportunity for the platform that solves it first.

What Leti Introduces

Leti doesn't just list properties — it builds the entire transaction infrastructure:

  • Escrow-secured transactions on every booking and service
  • Verified hosts, guests, and service providers with KYC
  • Integrated Service Hub — cleaners, chefs, plumbers, and more
  • Structured onboarding for property owners and providers
  • Live tracking, digital records, and dispute resolution

We're not just a rental platform. We're building the infrastructure layer for short-lets and home services in Nigeria.

💰 Revenue Model

Five Streams of Recurring Revenue

Leti earns at every touchpoint in the ecosystem — from bookings to services to featured placements.

1

Booking Commission

5–12% commission on every short-let booking processed through the platform. As properties grow, this scales automatically.

2

Escrow Processing Fees

A secure payment handling fee on every transaction — bookings and services alike. Protection generates revenue.

3

Service Hub Registration

₦3,000 one-time early access fee per service provider. Scales as we onboard thousands of professionals across Lagos.

4

Service Transaction Commission

10% commission on every completed cleaning, chef, plumbing, and maintenance job. Recurring demand means recurring income.

5

Premium Listings

Property owners and service providers can pay for featured placement and priority visibility in search results.

The Flywheel

More properties attract more guests. More guests need more services. More services bring more providers. Every layer compounds revenue.

⚙️ The Revenue Engine

Why the Service Hub Is the Key

The Service Hub creates a transaction loop inside the ecosystem. Every booking triggers service demand — and every service earns Leti commission.

Every booking needs cleaning → Many bookings need chefs → Maintenance is constant → Owners prefer verified vendors
More properties on Leti = more service demand.
More service demand = more commission revenue.
