End-to-End Short-Let Services

From consultation to your first guest — here's the full journey with Leti Stay Management.

Property Sourcing

🔑 Smart Lock Technology

The Key to Self-Managed Properties

The Leti Smart Lock eliminates the need for physical key handoffs. Manage your property's access from anywhere in the world — securely and automatically.

Generate timed passwords — create a unique code for each guest that expires when their booking ends

One-time access codes — guest uses it once to enter, and the code is permanently invalidated after

Revoke access instantly — invalidate any password from your phone, anywhere in the world

Multiple access methods — password, physical key backup, and card reader all built into one device

Long-lasting battery — charges once and lasts months. Works on your existing door with no major renovation
