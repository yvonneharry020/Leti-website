⚠️ The Problem

The Short-Let Industry Is Broken

If you own or manage a short-let property in Nigeria, you already know the daily frustrations that come with it.

What You're Dealing With Right Now

As a property owner in Nigeria's short-let market, these problems aren't new to you:

  • Guests cancel at the last minute and you lose income with no protection
  • Agents sit between you and your money, delaying payments for days or weeks
  • Fraud and fake bank transfers are a constant risk with no verification system
  • Finding reliable cleaners and maintenance workers is a never-ending headache
  • You're managing bookings through WhatsApp, calls, and scattered spreadsheets
  • Multiple agents list your property at wildly different prices — destroying your brand

Most property owners are running their short-let business without any real structure. No verification. No payment protection. No accountability.

Leti changes all of that.
